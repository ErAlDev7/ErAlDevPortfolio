## Name and a short description: E-Raporto;  Description: A citizen problem-reporting platform for The Municipalities Of Albania. A citizen photographs a pothole, a broken streetlight, an overflowing bin — the system classifies it with AI, checks it against nearby open reports so the same pothole doesn't become forty tickets, routes it to the right municipal department and person, and pushes every status change back to the citizen and the staff dashboard in real time.

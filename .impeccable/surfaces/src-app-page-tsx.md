# Surface brief: ErAlDev portfolio (home and project pages)

Mode: Experience (portfolio). Real screenshots lead; the interface recedes.

Audience: recruiters, hiring managers, and prospective freelance or agency clients, skimming for proof of capability.
Action: open a project, then write to the company (mailto only; no backend).
Proof: six real projects with screenshots; no invented metrics, clients or testimonials.
Constraints: Next.js static export; logo traced from a JPEG; self-hosted fonts; AA contrast both themes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A specification sheet, not a landing page. Work is a ruled register of real projects with specs (type, year, status, stack), and a sticky preview that follows the focused row. Refuses the centered hero plus card grid plus icon-in-circle template.

OWN-WORLD: Ink-blue neutrals tinted toward the logo hue (#0b1118 dark, #f4f6f8 light), one chromatic color (logo blue #1e7fcd, text-safe variants per theme), hairline rules, square corners (0 on containers, 2px inputs, 6px screenshot frames, 22px device frames), a slanted 60-degree cut on primary buttons echoing the A in the logo. Schibsted Grotesk for all text, JetBrains Mono only for stacks, years and measurements.

STORY: A visitor learns in one viewport that ErAlDev builds websites, mobile apps and backends, sees real work beside the statement, scans the register, opens a project to inspect every screenshot, and emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, a 4.75rem statement "We build websites, mobile apps and the backends behind them." with lede, primary "See the work" (slanted cut) and a text link. Right, E-Raporto website in a browser frame with its mobile app overlapping bottom-left, bleeding past the content edge. Stack line in mono under a hairline.

FORM: Experience portfolio as register; direction pinned by the user approving PLAN.md, so the concept roll was not run. seed: none (roll not run)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

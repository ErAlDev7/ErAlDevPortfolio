# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Next.js (React 19) with TypeScript, static export. Plain CSS with design tokens; no CSS framework. Deployable to Vercel, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages.

## Users
Two audiences: recruiters and hiring managers evaluating the owner for jobs, and prospective freelance or agency clients deciding whether to hire ErAlDev. Both skim quickly and want proof of capability.

## Product Purpose
The portfolio site of ErAlDev, a small software company that builds websites, mobile apps and the backends behind them. It exists to turn visitors into job offers and client inquiries. Success: a visitor understands what ErAlDev builds, sees real work, and writes to the company.

## Positioning
A full-stack team that answers for the whole product (interface, mobile app, server), shown through six real projects. No claim beyond what the projects demonstrate.

## Operating Context
Contact is by email only (mailto, no backend): eraldev7@gmail.com, plus Instagram @eraldev7. Work so far serves users in Albania and Germany.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Stacks in delivered projects: React, React Native, TypeScript, C# Web API, PostgreSQL; two sites started in AI builders (Lovable, v0).
- KthemaGati has no screenshots; it gets a text-only page.
- SmartBus Albania is dated 2025; no founding-year claim is made.

## Brand Commitments
Name "ErAlDev". Logo: white E, A, D monogram with a rising arrow, blue (#1e7fcd sampled) A and "Dev", tagline "Websites | Software | IT". The logo artwork on the site is traced from a JPEG and is a stand-in for the original vector.

## Evidence on Hand
Six projects with screenshots (48 in total) and descriptions in assets/projects: E-Raporto, KthemaGati (no media), NestAlbania, E&A Clean Service, Qendra Kazus, SmartBus Albania. No testimonials, client logos or metrics exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles
- Real work over claims: every capability is backed by a concrete project.
- Serve both audiences on one site: a hirer and a client should each find their answer fast.
- Contact is always one step away.
- Truthful content only; drafts are flagged as drafts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast (body text AAA), full keyboard use, reduced-motion support, visible focus.
